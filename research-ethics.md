The study was supported by the Iran University of Medical Sciences (IUMS) under grant number:

**IUMS/SHMIS-98-1-37-14546**

Ethical approval was granted by the Research Ethics Committee of the Iran University of Medical Sciences under approval number:

**IR.IUMS.REC.1398.274**

## Research Context

This study formed part of a Ph.D. dissertation supported by the Iran University of Medical Sciences (IUMS).

The research focused on the development and evaluation of a knowledge representation framework for pediatric patient safety and contributed to the broader doctoral research programme underlying the Onto_PPS project.

---

## Intellectual Property and Research Material Availability
