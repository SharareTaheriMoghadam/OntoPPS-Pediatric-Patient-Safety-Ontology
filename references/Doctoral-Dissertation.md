# Doctoral Dissertation

## Onto_PPS Doctoral Research

The Onto_PPS research was conducted as part of a Ph.D. dissertation in Medical Informatics at the Iran University of Medical Sciences (IUMS).

The complete doctoral dissertation is not publicly distributed in this repository.

The dissertation is available through the institutional library system of the Iran University of Medical Sciences and the library of the School of Health Management and Information Sciences, subject to the applicable institutional access policies.

---

## Dissertation Research

The doctoral research focused on the development and evaluation of a knowledge representation framework for pediatric patient safety.

The research programme included:

- systematic investigation of patient safety classifications, taxonomies, and ontologies;
- analysis of ontology development and evaluation methodologies;
- investigation of patient safety knowledge coverage;
- extraction and organization of pediatric patient safety knowledge;
- ontology engineering and knowledge representation;
- multi-dimensional ontology evaluation.
