# Research Publications

The following scholarly publications were produced from, or directly contributed to, the doctoral research underlying the Onto_PPS project.

---

## 1. Patient Safety Classifications, Taxonomies and Ontologies: A Systematic Review on Development and Evaluation Methodologies

**Taheri Moghadam S, Hooman N, Sheikhtaheri A.**

This systematic review investigated the methodologies used to develop and evaluate patient safety classifications, taxonomies, and ontologies, providing an evidence-based foundation for the development and evaluation framework of the Onto_PPS research.

🔗 [Read the article on ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1532046422001629)

---

## 2. Patient Safety Classifications for Health Information Technology (HIT) and Medical Devices: A Review on Available Systems

**Taheri Moghadam S, Hooman N, Sheikhtaheri A.**

This review examined available patient safety classification systems relevant to Health Information Technology (HIT) and medical devices, contributing to the broader knowledge synthesis underlying the Onto_PPS research programme.

🔗 [Read the article on PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35592975/)

---

## 3. Patient Safety Classifications, Taxonomies and Ontologies, Part 2: A Systematic Review on Content Coverage

**Taheri Moghadam S, Hooman N, Sheikhtaheri A.**

This systematic review investigated the content coverage of existing patient safety classifications, taxonomies, and ontologies, supporting the assessment of conceptual coverage and knowledge representation in the Onto_PPS research.

🔗 [Read the article on ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1532046423002708)

---

## Relationship to the Onto_PPS Doctoral Research

Together, these publications represent the broader scholarly research programme that supported the development of Onto_PPS, an ontology for pediatric patient safety.

The publications contributed to:

- understanding existing patient safety knowledge representation systems;
- identifying methodological approaches for ontology development and evaluation;
- analysing the content coverage of existing patient safety classifications and ontologies;
- informing the conceptual scope and evaluation strategy of the Onto_PPS research.

> **Note:** The Onto_PPS ontology artifact and the full doctoral thesis are not publicly distributed in this repository. This repository serves as a research portfolio and project showcase presenting a high-level overview of the research, methodology, evaluation framework, scientific contributions, and scholarly outputs.
