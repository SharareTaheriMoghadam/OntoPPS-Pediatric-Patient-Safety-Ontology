# Scientific Contributions

## Overview

The Onto_PPS doctoral research contributed to the fields of Medical Informatics, Biomedical Informatics, Patient Safety, Knowledge Representation, and Ontology Engineering.

---

## 1. Pediatric Patient Safety Knowledge Representation

The research developed a formal knowledge representation approach for pediatric patient safety.

The project addressed the need to organize heterogeneous patient safety knowledge within a structured conceptual framework.

---

## 2. Integration of Fragmented Patient Safety Knowledge

The research integrated knowledge derived from:

- scientific literature;
- patient safety classifications;
- taxonomies;
- ontologies;
- healthcare knowledge representation systems.

This integration supported a more structured representation of pediatric patient safety knowledge.
