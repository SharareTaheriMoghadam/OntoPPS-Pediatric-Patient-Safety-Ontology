# Knowledge Integration and Interoperability

## Overview

A central component of the Onto_PPS research was the integration of heterogeneous patient safety knowledge from multiple sources.

Patient safety knowledge is distributed across scientific literature, classifications, taxonomies, ontologies, reporting systems, and clinical knowledge resources. These systems may differ in terminology, conceptual structure, scope, and level of detail.

The Onto_PPS research addressed this fragmentation through a structured knowledge integration approach.

---

## Knowledge Sources

The research incorporated knowledge from multiple sources, including:

- pediatric patient safety literature;
- existing patient safety classifications;
- patient safety taxonomies;
- biomedical ontologies;
- healthcare knowledge representation systems;
- domain-specific terminology and conceptual frameworks.
