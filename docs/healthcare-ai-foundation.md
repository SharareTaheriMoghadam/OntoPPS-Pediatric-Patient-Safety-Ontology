# Foundation for Healthcare Artificial Intelligence

## Overview

The Onto_PPS doctoral research established a methodological foundation for subsequent research at the intersection of structured medical knowledge and artificial intelligence.

Ontology engineering provides a formal approach for representing concepts, relationships, and domain knowledge. This makes ontology-based knowledge particularly relevant to modern healthcare AI systems.

---
## Conceptual Alignment

The Onto_PPS research was designed around the principle that patient safety knowledge should be systematically organized and represented in a structured, semantically meaningful way.

The research investigated and integrated knowledge from heterogeneous sources, including:

- scientific literature;
- patient safety classifications;
- taxonomies;
- ontologies;
- healthcare knowledge representation systems.

This conceptual alignment supported the organization of related patient safety knowledge and provided a structured foundation for subsequent computational applications.

At a high level, the conceptual approach can be represented as:

                ▼
        Concept Identification

                ▼

## Interoperability Perspective

Formal knowledge representation can support the integration of heterogeneous healthcare knowledge sources.

By organizing concepts and semantic relationships within a structured knowledge model, ontology-based approaches can provide a foundation for:

- semantic interoperability;
- knowledge integration;
- cross-system information retrieval;
- patient safety data analysis;
- clinical knowledge management;
- integration of structured knowledge with healthcare information systems.
