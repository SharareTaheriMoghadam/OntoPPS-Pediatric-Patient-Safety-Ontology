## Evaluation Framework

Ontology quality cannot be adequately assessed through a single metric or evaluation method.

The Onto_PPS research therefore adopted a multi-dimensional evaluation framework combining complementary approaches to assess the ontology from different perspectives.

The evaluation framework included:

### Expert-Based Evaluation

Domain experts were involved in assessing the ontology according to structured evaluation criteria.

This approach provided a human and domain-oriented assessment of the ontology's:

- relevance;
- appropriateness;
- clarity;
- organization;
- usefulness;
- applicability to pediatric patient safety.

---

### Literature-Based Content Coverage

The ontology was evaluated against concepts identified from relevant pediatric patient safety literature.

This approach examined the extent to which the ontology represented concepts identified in the scientific literature.

The evaluation focused on:

- concept coverage;
- representation of relevant patient safety knowledge;
- identification of potential knowledge gaps.

---

### Comparative Evaluation

The ontology was compared with relevant existing patient safety classifications, taxonomies, and ontologies.

The comparison considered characteristics including:

- conceptual scope;
- concept organization;
- semantic relationships;
- hierarchical structure;
- domain coverage;
- alignment with existing knowledge systems.

---

### Computational and Structural Evaluation

Computational methods were used to investigate structural properties of the ontology.

This component examined characteristics including:

- consistency;
- coherence;
- structural organization;
- logical quality.
